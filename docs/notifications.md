# Alerts

Cloudflare Alerts monitor your account and notify you when something needs attention — from DDoS attacks and certificate expirations to origin failures and billing spikes. Built-in alerts cover products such as DDoS Protection, SSL/TLS, and Billing. Custom Alerts let you define your own threshold, anomaly, or SLO conditions on supported SQL API datasets.

## Delivery methods

Alerts can be delivered to:

  * **Email** (all plans)
  * **[Webhooks](https://developers.cloudflare.com/notifications/get-started/configure-webhooks/)** (all plans; accounts without a paid zone can configure up to 100 webhook destinations)
  * **[PagerDuty](https://developers.cloudflare.com/notifications/get-started/configure-pagerduty/)** (Business and higher)
  * **Connected services** , including coding agents
